## About&nbsp;Me

Hi, I'm **Syifa** — a Software Engineering student from Indonesia, learning to build things that quietly work well instead of things that shout for attention.

Most of my time goes into **backend development**, learning how systems think, how data moves, and how logic can be both correct and kind. I'm also slowly finding my way into **full stack development**, one small project at a time.

Outside of code, I read and I write. I think too much about psychology — why people do what they do — and about philosophy, which rarely gives answers but always gives better questions.

I like rainy afternoons, coffee going cold beside my laptop, and silence that isn't empty, just resting.

I'd rather build something meaningful in the quiet than something loud that fades by winter.
