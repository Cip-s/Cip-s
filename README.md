## 🍂 About Me

<img align="right" width="230" src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/rahuldkjain/github-profile-readme-generator@master/src/assets/coding.gif" alt="coding gif" />

I'm **Syifa**, a Software Engineering student from Indonesia, learning to build things that quietly work well — not things that shout for attention.

I spend most of my time around the **backend**, learning how systems think, how data moves, and how logic can be both correct and kind. I'm also slowly making my way into **Full Stack Development**, one small project at a time.

Outside of code, I read. I write. I think too much about psychology, about why people do what they do, and about philosophy, which rarely gives answers but always gives better questions.

I like rainy afternoons, the smell of coffee going cold beside my laptop, and the kind of silence that isn't empty — it's just resting.

I'd rather build something meaningful in the quiet than something loud that fades by winter.
